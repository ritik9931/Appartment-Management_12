A Building With History · Established in 1997

A Building With History, A Community With Heart

Radha Krishna Tower is a well-established residential apartment located on Captain Veera Raja Reddy Marg, Habsiguda, Hyderabad. Constructed in 1997, the building has been a part of the Habsiguda community for nearly three decades and continues to provide a convenient residential location for its owners and families.

Constructed in 1997, Radha Krishna Tower has witnessed the growth and transformation of Habsiguda over the years. As the neighbourhood has developed, the surrounding infrastructure and availability of essential services have also expanded. Today, Radha Krishna Tower represents not only a residential building but also a community with a long history, established neighbourhood, and convenient urban location.

Location & Conveniences

A Convenient & Well-Connected Location

One of the major advantages of Radha Krishna Tower is its prime Habsiguda location. Offering a harmonious blend of residential comfort, essential services, transportation, and connectivity — day-to-day life is convenient for families, senior citizens, and working professionals alike.
